### Pre-registered reading (ADR-0019), applied to the generated tables

- **E1 < E0 by more than the seed spread on both corpora: met.** Mean over seeds of E1 − E0 on
  tune-B is −0.060 on SciFact (E0 spread 0.013) and −0.011 on NFCorpus (E0 spread 0.007). Under
  the same information source, linear capacity, and optimization budget, the mask-average target
  trains a worse selector than the graded L2S target.
- **E2 recovers E0 − E1: yes, fully, on both corpora.** E2 − E0 is +0.007 to +0.008 on SciFact
  and +0.002 to +0.006 on NFCorpus; 5 of 6 intervals include zero (NFCorpus seed 0:
  [+0.0001, +0.0118]). Correction 2026-11-15: this does not show that the per-budget masks carry
  as much information "once the loss does not average them". E2 changes the loss and the target
  presentation together, so the recovery is not attributed to either. ADR-0020 tests them
  separately.
- **E3 − E3c: no separation.** E3 and E3c both stay at E1's level (SciFact −0.064 to −0.073
  against −0.058 to −0.064; NFCorpus −0.009 to −0.016 against −0.007 to −0.015). A per-budget
  target behind a budget-input offset did not recover anything. That suggests, without
  establishing it, that E2's recovery is not due to per-budget targets under KL or to the extra
  parameters. A loss effect still needs ADR-0020's matched contrasts.
- **E4 (λ = 0.1 on tune-A in both corpora): not a candidate.** The rule needs ≥ +0.005 over E0 in
  both corpora; SciFact gives −0.013 to −0.028 (two intervals below zero), NFCorpus +0.002 to
  +0.006.
- E2 is not covered by a candidate rule. Read with the E4 rule by analogy, it would also fail:
  its NFCorpus mean gain is 0.004 < 0.005. It is a development observation only.

Limits: two corpora; SciFact is development data whose F12 evaluation was seen; tune-B is small
(61 queries on SciFact); three seeds. E2 differs from E1 in two ways at once: it uses the
per-budget masks without averaging, and it uses a rank-threshold BCE in place of KL. The pilot
does not separate these. Any follow-up needs a separate protocol on untouched data.
