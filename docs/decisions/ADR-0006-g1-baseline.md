# ADR-0006: Gate G1 — baseline reproduction

- **Status:** proposed (the researcher decides the outcome below before merge)
- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Issue:** F3 (`specs/004-l2s-baseline`), experiment E0

## Context

G1 asks whether the mask pipeline is correct and whether the Learning-to-Select baseline learns its
target before selector shift is tested (research R7). E0 ran on SciFact, NFCorpus, and MS MARCO
(with TREC DL 2019, DL 2020, and DL-HARD) on DataPolicy-4090 at commit `d2633a6`; the report is
`reports/E0/README.md`, generated from result files and regenerated byte-identically.

## Evidence

All values are in `reports/E0/README.md` ("Gate G1", "Learning-to-Select format", "Paired
tests", "Selector selection", and the DIME tables).

- **(b) masks are correct**: k = 1024 reproduces the unmasked rankings for every method on all six
  query sets. Met.
- **(c) ordering**: nDCG@10 at k = 256 orders oracle ≥ selector ≥ random in all three
  environments. Met.
- **(d) generated**: the report regenerates byte-identically. Met.
- **(a) the selector learns its target**: met on SciFact and MS MARCO; **not met on NFCorpus**.
  NFCorpus's validation KL is above the uniform predictor's at the chosen temperature, and in fact
  the selector beats a uniform guess only at τ = 0.005, and only by 1.6% (the per-cell values are
  in the official selection file of `artifacts/selectors/official/nfcorpus/`). Its retrieval still
  improves with training (validation nDCG@10 rises over epochs) and on test it matches full
  1024 and beats prefix and random significantly.
- **Context for F4**: at 30%, the selector beats full 1024 on SciFact (Holm-significant), ties it on
  NFCorpus, and is below it on MS MARCO dev, while beating prefix, random, and global importance on
  all three. The ADR-0003 candidates gained significantly over the official selector only on
  SciFact. Oracle headroom is large everywhere.

## Decision

To be chosen by the researcher. Options:

1. **G1 fails as written** (criterion (a) on NFCorpus). Record the evidence, tag
   `g1-baseline-valid` is not applied, and stop before F4 (constitution IV: a failed gate is not
   answered by adding complexity).
2. **G1 passes with NFCorpus flagged.** Read (a) as intended — the selector learns something its
   target asks for — and accept retrieval evidence for NFCorpus, recording that its linear selector
   does not fit the oracle distribution. F4 proceeds on all three environments with NFCorpus's
   selector-shift results interpreted with that caveat.
3. **G1 passes on SciFact and MS MARCO only.** F4 proceeds without NFCorpus as an in-domain
   reference, and NFCorpus returns only as a later environment.

Options 2 and 3 amend how research R7's criterion (a) is applied after seeing the results, so the
amendment is recorded here and carries the `protocol-change` label.

## Consequences

- Options 2 and 3 require tagging the merge `g1-baseline-valid` and starting F4.
- Option 1 closes later milestones as not planned, with this evidence kept.
