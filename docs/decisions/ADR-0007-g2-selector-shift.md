# ADR-0007: Gate G2 — selector shift (H1)

- **Status:** accepted (by the researcher, 2026-10-15)
- **Date:** 2026-10-14
- **Issue:** #20, F4 (`specs/005-selector-shift`), experiment E1

## Context

G2 asks whether selectors trained on different environments select different dimensions
beyond seed-to-seed variation, and whether that costs retrieval when a selector is moved
(research R8). If not, the hypernetwork is not needed. This draft is generated by
`hyperdime decide G2` from the result files of `reports/E1/README.md`.

## Evidence

From transfer-matrix `20261014T021651Z-08a6e7d9` (commit `8f2d939309a6`); every value is in
`reports/E1/README.md`.

- **Criterion 1. cross-domain nDCG@10 significantly below in-domain (Holm, alpha 0.05)**: 5 of 6 ordered pairs; threshold >= 3 of 6; met.
- **Criterion 2. cross-environment overlap@k below within-environment (exact permutation)**: T = +0.4150, p = 0.0036 over 1680 assignments; threshold T > 0 and p <= 0.05; met.

Context, not decisive:

- **departure_0**: `msmarco`: F3 selected `global-regularized` (validation nDCG@10 0.3241); F4 uses `wide-grid` (0.3239), as the spec's clarification Q1 decided.
- **fit**: msmarco fits (gain +0.0320, Holm p 0.0003), nfcorpus fits (gain +0.0278, Holm p 0.0003), scifact fits (gain +0.1401, Holm p 0.0003)
- **fixed_subset**: in-domain selector significantly above a fixed subset in 9 of 9 comparisons
- **global_selector**: `global-selector` is F3's single selector trained on the training labels of all environments together (never on a test label), so it is not an in-domain baseline.
- **note_msmarco**: E0 does not reproduce Learning-to-Select on MS MARCO (ADR-0006). E1 scores MS MARCO dev against the full 8.8M-passage corpus; the paper evaluates the `test` split of an unpublished `msmarco_subset_50k` folder, with an ideal DCG taken from the retrieved list (F4 research D9).
- **note_nfcorpus**: NFCorpus's selector does not fit its oracle distribution as a calibrated distribution (ADR-0006); read its shift with its ranking-fit result (F4 research D2).
- **payoff**: in-domain selector significantly above the global selector in 2 of 3 comparisons

## Decision

Research R8's criteria read **pass**. Accepted by the researcher on 2026-10-15: **G2 passes**;
H1 (selector shift) is supported on SciFact, NFCorpus, and MS MARCO, with the global selector's
payoff and the MS MARCO and NFCorpus notes above as recorded context.
