## Decisions in §9

- **Development p-values.** Agree with dropping a veto at five families. For five nonzero effects the smallest one-sided sign-flip p is 1/32 and any negative effect leaves at least two assignments in the upper tail. Object to reinstating that veto at eight. Two outer-fold fits share K−2 training families, and increasing K leaves this dependence intact. The held-out outcomes can be concordant because of common fitted components. Counting sign patterns exactly does not make their probabilities equal. Use the retrieval and attribution screens at every K. Reserve binding inference for independently held families with a frozen procedure. A full refit under a descriptor shuffle addresses a different null and still requires exchangeable assignments.
- **Offset plus slope.** Agree as a single selection procedure, conditional on the F1 and F2 maps each having their own matched oracle, with the slope earning the registered 0.005 increment on inner held-family retrieval. The 90–96% and 98–100% fits describe three specialist *prior-share* curves. Prior share is a ratio of within-query logit spreads. Neither it nor the S4–S3 comparison measures the value of predicting a second coefficient for an unseen family. See `round4-opus-e.md:99–111`, `src/hyperdime/selection/diagnostics.py:23–30` and `docs/decisions/ADR-0012-s4-budget-prior.md` (ADR-0012, lines 9–12, 25–26).
- **Non-inferiority.** Agree with its omission. A mean superiority test against zero answers the registered primary question. Family-specific material harm has a different estimand, and low query counts can leave losses exceeding 0.010 compatible with the data. Treat absence of detected harm as unresolved wherever the simultaneous interval crosses the harm margin. Retain the veto on detected harm and report the workload-specific cost result independently.
- I also agree with P0 first, the fresh-G3 veto for coordinate models, and deferral of rank four. P1 on test-only collections is defensible with independently sourced unlabeled query IDs and a frozen complement. Eight to twelve convenience families can support a symmetry-qualified panel contrast. A population-frame statement additionally needs a documented selection design.
