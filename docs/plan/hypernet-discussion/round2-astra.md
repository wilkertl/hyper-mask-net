# Round 2 · Astra · Which part of a selector can an unseen corpus identify?

**Decision.** Allocate the next experiment to measuring headroom over a corpus-balanced global selector with target variance (G+V), followed by a scalar conditioning pilot if headroom survives. I withdraw rank four as the first candidate. The most informative negative result would be that supervised specialists still gain, yet a label-free corpus descriptor cannot predict even the direction in which to adjust the prior.

References to proposal lines below use the files in this directory. Empirical values come from repository results. Parameter counts are arithmetic. Thresholds below are proposed decision rules. The available corpus counts and prospective power are planning assumptions.

## What I concede

**The 4,186-parameter correction does not survive as a build decision at the anticipated corpus count.** More precisely, my P0 head *learns* 4,186 shared parameters and *generates* 5,120 numbers per corpus. Of the learned parameters, 4 × 1,024 = 4,096 define the shared query basis and 18 × 5 = 90 map descriptor rows to four loadings and one bias (round1-astra.md:24–38). Queries can constrain the shared basis and rows constrain the tied map. Neither supplies a new draw of the relationship between corpus geometry and relevance. Opus-M's roughly 8–10 **new labeled corpora before splitting** is an unaudited inventory estimate (round1-opus-m.md:170–201). It falls well short of a demonstrated pool for training a family-general residual. Opus-E's ≥20 is a reasonable resource screen for reconsideration, with separate validation and confirmation families still required. It gives no statistical guarantee.

**My architecture nests corpus-balanced G+V. Its claimed gain does not yet survive that comparator.** Setting the residual to zero preserves any G+V checkpoint. Equal initial predictions say nothing about out-of-family improvement. Round1-astra.md:43, 50 also samples families uniformly, which changes the base relative to uniform corpus sampling when family sizes differ. I accept uniform corpus sampling for the paired training arms, a family-balanced sensitivity analysis, and equal-family weighting for the generalization endpoint. If G+V absorbs the payoff, it is the result.

I retract my blanket G3 veto (round1-astra.md:42) and the choice of 307 as the primary endpoint (line 52). E2 tested coordinate-profile shift. It did not test a corpus-wide shift in prior strength. The nine-decile mean matches the budget-conditioned decision. Keep 307 for continuity with E1. I retain the frozen shared base, corpus-centered rows, bias-only comparison and donor swap that holds the recipient variance fixed.

## Four disagreements that change the experiment

1. **What counts as headroom?** Use cross-fitted specialist minus balanced G+V on development families. Report absolute gains, the fraction of families benefiting and uncertainty. An aggregate recovery fraction is interpretable only with a reliably positive denominator. E1 and F12 motivate the comparison but neither measured it.
2. **Which target must be predictable?** For the scalar, require out-of-family retrieval gain from predicting a single prior offset. For a bias, require fresh G3 evidence *and* improvement over the scalar. Neither a stable coefficient on a flat response surface nor accurate frequency prediction alone establishes value for ranking.
3. **What is an independent unit?** Training labels, held-out queries and independent parent collections answer different questions. Group shared documents, near duplicates and closely related query collections before splitting. Reserve disjoint families for confirmation. Synthetic episodes may be training perturbations only. If the inventory supports only exploratory folds, publish the pilot and its uncertainty.
4. **How much target information is warranted?** Start with P0 as the primary architectural test. P1 is a separately fitted, prespecified secondary arm on corpora with a disjoint unlabeled calibration pool. Compare P0 and P1 on the same eligible corpora. Treat per-query feedback and offline pseudo-query fitting as retrieval and cost competitors. Better P1 results alone would support adaptation to a query population, a narrower claim than adaptation from documents alone.

## Merged architecture and unlock rules

All gates below are prospective. Freeze family membership, data eligibility, thresholds, splits and compute allowance before inspecting fresh results. Use the three historical environments only for exploration or training. Within development, hold out whole families and refit the base, normalization and generator inside every outer fold. Use nested training folds for penalty selection. Obtain oracle fits, selection scores and scored gains on disjoint non-test queries. No specialist, oracle, pseudo-query tuner using judgments, or negative miner receives a meta-test label.

**Rung 0 — G+V.** Unlock once the split and resource audit identifies usable development families. Use frozen Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B, D = 1,024. Train one pooled S4 with uniform corpus sampling, then draw a query and budget within each episode. Use the existing masked retrieval loss, meta-train positives and training-only negatives. Freeze W [1,024 × 1,024], b [1,024] and nine nonnegative base weights a⁰. The coordinate logits are Wq + b + a⁰ₖ log(q² ⊙ v_T + ε), with target document variance v_T. Score documents exactly with the hard top-k mask applied to q, original document vectors and no masked-query normalization. Run legacy, balanced no-prior, pooled-variance, target-variance-only, full and prefix controls. Census specialist headroom against this frozen base.

**Rung 1 — one corpus offset.** Unlock a pilot if mean specialist headroom is ≥0.005 nDCG@10 and at least 30% of development families gain ≥0.005. With the base frozen, sweep a scalar t on a prespecified grid containing zero. Fit the *attainable* oracle aₖ(t) = max(0, a⁰ₖ + t) on development train queries, select on validation and score independently. Require mean attainable oracle gain ≥0.005 and ≥50% of positive aggregate headroom before fitting a predictor. Report each family's curve and split-half retrieval regret to distinguish an unstable optimum from many equally useful offsets.

Predict t = βᵀφ̃(T) using at most three slopes and training-fold standardization. For P0 use log corpus size, log covariance participation ratio and seeded mean pairwise document cosine. For P1 replace the last statistic with the calibration queries' mean top-one cosine. Fit ridge to the selected finite offsets with equal corpus weights and choose shrinkage by inner held-family retrieval. The projected additive link reproduces G+V exactly at β = 0, permits zero weights and can reactivate them. A common shift tests one specific hypothesis about budgets. Report the unrestricted F9 oracle alongside it to expose missed budget interactions.

Advance the scalar to locked validation only if outer held-family mean gain is ≥0.005 over G+V, recovers ≥40% of its own attainable oracle gain, exceeds the 95th percentile of whole-family descriptor permutations with refitting, and matches or beats nearest-corpus and size-only prediction. Keep v_T correct in every swap. If the permutation design lacks enough independent exchanges, record a pilot. If scalar utility is absent, keep G+V. A larger model requires its own positive oracle and predictability evidence.

**Rung 2 — tied coordinate bias.** Unlock only if development leaves ≥0.005 mean incremental oracle bias gain above the attainable scalar oracle, a fresh unchanged E2/G3 shift probe meets the registered criteria (ADR-0013), and fresh families remain for validation. Add X_T w, with X_T [1,024 × h] centered at each coordinate's meta-train mean, w [h] initialized to zero, h = 18 for the existing P0 descriptor. Freeze the accepted lower rung and train w with its retrieval objective. Compare against that lower rung, a constant-template control with matched updates, and a donor-row swap. A directly applied linear feature term and a cached generated bias are algebraically identical. Credit any gain to the descriptor features. Require ≥0.005 incremental held-family gain and a positive correct-minus-donor gain on locked validation to advance.

**Rung 3 — query-dependent rank four, deferred.** Reopen only with ≥20 audited real meta-train environments, enough independent validation and test families for the locked decision, and ≥0.005 mean incremental held-family oracle gain above the bias rung. Measure this oracle using the actual shared query basis learned without the held family, then fit only the allowed output-side correction on that family's development training queries. A free per-family two-factor oracle is too permissive. Add (X_T H)(Bq), H [h × 4] initially zero, B [4 × 1,024] initially orthonormal. Freeze the lower rung and compare the learned basis against a fixed random basis as well as bias-only, matched global and donor controls. Require ≥0.005 incremental validation gain and positive correct-minus-donor gain. With today's inventory this rung stays closed.

**One final evaluation.** Select and freeze the simplest qualifying rung before opening meta-test judgments. Evaluate equal-family mean paired exact nDCG@10 gain averaged over the nine deciles. Require a point gain ≥0.005 over G+V and positive lower 95% family-level bounds both against G+V and against the donor control. A claim of an established minimum gain of 0.005 requires the G+V lower bound itself to exceed that margin. Simulate the joint decision using development-family variability and within-family paired query uncertainty, including asymmetric effects. Require planned power ≥80% at a prespecified alternative of +0.010 for both contrasts or label the evaluation exploratory. Report family effects, leave-one-family-out sensitivity, harm intervals and Holm-adjusted secondary per-corpus comparisons. An upper bound below +0.005 retires the practical-gain claim. A wider interval leaves it unresolved.

Record offline descriptor construction, generation and any pseudo-query training separately from online encoding, selector, mask and exact retrieval. Compare feedback baselines at equal budget and report the extra search pass. Preserve manifests with every checkpoint and result. No proposed threshold is an observed outcome.
