# Round 1 · Astra · Generate four corrections to a pooled selector

**Build only after a fresh G3 replication.** My candidate is a P0 hypernetwork that reads how each coordinate's corpus statistics depart from its usual values and emits four loadings and one bias for that coordinate. The loadings tell a pooled selector how to respond to four shared projections of a new query. Two queries on the same corpus can therefore change masks in different directions. The cheaper competing explanation is that a single global selector already captures everything transferable once it has access to document variance. That is the primary comparison.

## What the evidence buys

The following numbers were checked against the named project tables or reports. I did not recompute them from embeddings or reproduce the experiments. All numerical settings and decision thresholds below are **proposed**, and sizes and costs are **algebraic**, unless explicitly attributed to a result. Literature scope is the supplied local collection, with PDF page numbers counting covers. These sources motivate mechanisms and controls but supply no measured effect size for this experiment.

- At 307 coordinates, E1's in-domain minus pooled nDCG@10 was +0.1116 on SciFact, +0.0171 on NFCorpus and −0.0019 on MS MARCO. Five of six ordered cross-domain transfers degraded significantly. This establishes specialization on these workloads. It leaves open how much specialization a label-free procedure can recover. MS MARCO used full-corpus dev and NFCorpus had poor oracle calibration. Sources: `reports/E1/tables/payoff.csv`, rows 1–3, `reports/E1/tables/g2.csv`, criterion 1, and `reports/E1/README.md`, Notes.
- The decisive E2 shift probe failed in **all 18** environment-by-feature-set cells. On SciFact, adding all P1 features reached Spearman 0.4623 versus identity 0.4354 and variance 0.5381. On MS MARCO a direct variance probe reached 0.7864, yet the corresponding shift probe reached only 0.5657 against variance 0.6687. Predicting which coordinates are generally useful is easier here than predicting which ones change. A generator can cheaply encode the former and appear adaptive. Sources: `reports/E2/tables/criteria.csv`, sets 1–6, `reports/E2/tables/ladder.csv`, B1/B2 and sets 1/6, and ADR-0013 SC-001/002.
- S1 improved on L2S on the budget-averaged endpoint in two of three environments. S2 introduced the variance prior, S3 a learned coefficient, and S4 one coefficient per budget. S4 beat aligned S3 in two of three environments. Its prior share at 307 was 0.9512 on MS MARCO, 0.4281 on NFCorpus and 0.3580 on SciFact. This motivates giving the **control itself** the target corpus variance and budget coefficients. Sources: `reports/S1/README.md`, SC-001, `reports/S2/README.md` and `reports/S3/README.md`, opening equations, `reports/S4/tables/s4.csv`, SC-002, and ADR-0012, prior_share.
- Privileged masks reveal a training signal but their objective matters. On the same NFCorpus training queries, transferring one positive's mask to another at 307 incurred 0.3321 normalized regret versus 0.1292 with all positives visible. This is similarity regret. In P3-objective, direct ranking R0 gained 0.0190 versus REF on SciFact and 0.0007 on NFCorpus on reused tune-B. Hence use multi-positive ranking as a controlled objective and measure actual full-corpus retrieval. Neither P3 observation warrants fitting to held-out oracle masks. Sources: `reports/P3-shared-mask/README.md`, NFCorpus transfer at 307, ADR-0014–0017, `reports/P3-objective/README.md`, R0, and ADR-0019–0020. The benchmark freeze in `reports/P3-bench/RUNS.md`, F12, supplies no held-out outcome.

**Architectural precedent.** *Learning to Select: Query-Aware Adaptive Dimension Selection for Dense Retrieval* (p. 1) motivates predicting coordinate importance from the query. *Dimension Importance Estimation for Dense Information Retrieval* (p. 2), *Unveiling DIME* (p. 2) and *Eclipse* (p. 1) motivate query–document contributions and a pseudo-feedback alternative. *Statistical Foundations of DIME* (p. 1) treats the choice of pruning index as its own estimation problem. *When Reducing Representations Improves Performance* (p. 1) challenges independent-dimension selection. A low-rank correction is a deliberately limited test of cross-coordinate dependence.

*HyperNetworks* (p. 1) motivates generating parameters, and *Generalization Bounds via Meta-Learned Model Representations* (p. 1, Fig. 1) depicts dataset-to-predictor generation. Its bound does not certify this design. *Principled Weight Initialization for Hypernetworks* (p. 1) motivates checking the scale of the generated target weights. *Hypencoder: Hypernetworks for Information Retrieval* and *Hypencoder Revisited* (both p. 1) generate a query-specific nonlinear document scorer. Here the generated object is a selector used before scoring. *Search-Adaptor* and *Align then Train* (both p. 1) motivate adaptation around embedding models. *SPIN* (p. 1) selects internal neurons for classification, a different intervention. *AdaWidth* (p. 1) varies width by query. Following the distinction between control target and control mechanism in *A Survey of Controllable Learning* (p. 1), hold width fixed within each comparison.

## From an unlabeled corpus to one selector checkpoint

Let D = 1024. Keep the project's frozen, normalized, last-token Qwen3 embeddings and instruction convention. For P0, use the existing matrix F_T of shape [D,18] with document marginals, tails and redundancy (groups A/E). For the separately fitted P1 arm use [D,43], adding query marginals, interaction and exact top-20 versus seeded background statistics. Reserve up to 128 unlabeled, hash-selected calibration queries from a non-evaluation pool before any query-level split or judgment lookup. A missing calibration pool makes only P0 eligible. Declare eligibility before inspecting scores. P2 requires labels and has no arm here. Sources: `docs/protocols.md`, P0–P2 and leakage rules 1–5, `src/hyperdime/space/descriptor.py`, FEATURES and build_descriptors, and `docs/architecture.md`, F10.

For each feature j and coordinate i, compute M_ij as the equal-environment mean of F_eij on meta-train. Let s_j be the RMS of F_eij − M_ij over meta-train environments and coordinates. Freeze both with the model. Set X_ij = clip((F_Tij − M_ij)/max(s_j,1e−6), −5, 5). Save clipping fractions per column. No corpus names, IDs, counts, qrels, selector fingerprints or trainable dimension-ID vectors enter the generator. The row correspondence comes from the frozen encoder. The absolute target variance v_Ti is the square of its document standard deviation, available to every geometric arm. This separation lets the test distinguish a familiar dimension's variance from a departure that predicts a change in how queries use it (ADR-0013, B1/B2).

Use column-vector queries. For feature width h and rank r = 4, the full model is

    H ∈ R^{h × (r+1)},  B ∈ R^{r × D}
    [A_T | c_T] = X_T H                  [D,r] and [D,1]
    ΔW_T = A_T B                         [D,D], rank ≤ 4
    g_i(q,T) = log(q_i² v_Ti + 1e−12)   [D]
    u(q,T,k) = W₀q + b₀ + a_k g(q,T) + A_T(Bq) + c_T
    m_i = 1{i in TopK(u,k)},            m ∈ {0,1}^D
    score(q,d) = Σ_i m_i q_i d_i.

W₀ is [1024,1024], b₀ is [1024], and a is nine nonnegative shared budget weights. Initialize W₀,b₀ at zero and a at one, pretrain this pooled S4 base, then freeze it for the adaptation experiment. Initialize H at zero and B with four seeded random orthonormal rows. The first forward pass exactly reproduces pooled S4. H can receive gradients on step one because B is nonzero. B starts learning once A_T moves. At the mean descriptor X=0 the base is recovered for every query, even after training. Training uses the same B for all corpora. The shared input factor constrains the proposed correction to reweight the same query directions on an unseen corpus. A free bias may change corpus-wide usage, so a bias-only ablation is decisive for the proposed query interaction (implementation plan M7–M8, ADR-0012).

Exact counts: base D²+D+9 = 1,049,609. Additional trainable parameters are rD+h(r+1), or 4,186 for P0 and 4,311 for P1. Totals are 1,053,795 and 1,053,920 respectively. Per corpus, generate 5,120 coefficients (20 KiB in float32), retain variance (4 KiB) and reference the shared weights. M and s are frozen preprocessing buffers, respectively Dh and h scalars. Store named descriptor columns, normalization and calibration hashes, frozen base hash, H/B hash, environment, rank and seed. Write the descriptor and generated checkpoint with run manifests using `src/hyperdime/contracts/schemas.py` (SpaceDescriptor v0, SelectorCheckpoint v0, kind generated) and `src/hyperdime/contracts/manifests.py` (ArtifactWriter).

**Why this restriction?** Generating both factors independently could rotate the input subspace on each corpus. Generating a dense map could express arbitrary changes but emits 1,048,576 matrix entries before bias, versus 5,120 entries here. Both alternatives make identification from few independent environments harder. Even the proposed model fails whenever two environments have the same chosen statistics and require different corrections. P1 tests the particular case of different query populations over similar documents. A single generated prior coefficient per budget is a credible simpler alternative: compare it explicitly before attributing gains to cross-coordinate corrections (implementation plan M7, ADR-0008, ADR-0012–0013).

## Training and measurement

1. **Unlock.** Repeat E2 unchanged on fresh development environments under issue #60, using the registered SC-001/002 baselines, seed checks and thresholds. Keep these environments out of final confirmation. A repeated failure stops the generator program. If only P1 qualifies, register P1 as primary before any retrieval evaluation on new environments. Never promote the direct probe to the gate. Preserve the original three corpora for exploratory development (ADR-0013, SC-001/002).
2. **Episodes.** Split by parent corpus before any fit, placing instruction variants, overlapping collections and shards in one family. On meta-train sample a family uniformly, then an environment uniformly, 32 training queries and one budget uniformly from {102,204,307,409,512,614,716,819,921}. Precompute descriptor support separately from supervised episode queries. Use only the environment's train qrels for graded positives and its frozen train-query negative pool from exact dense rankings, excluding all known positives. Reuse identical examples, pools, update counts, seeds and budget draws in every paired arm. Audit overlap by ID and content hash. No target-time optimizer, oracle or negative pool. Sources: `docs/protocols.md`, Splits and rules 1–5, `src/hyperdime/training/retrieval_trainer.py`, BUDGETS.
3. **Objective.** Reuse the project's soft mask during training: standardize u over coordinates, subtract the detached midpoint between its kth and (k+1)th order statistics, then apply sigmoid after division by β. For candidate multiset C_q (all positives, sampled hard negatives and distinct other in-batch positives excluding own positives), let l_d = ⟨q ⊙ softmask,d⟩/0.05. Minimize the query mean of logsumexp over C_q minus Σ_p w_p l_p, with positive grades normalized to sum to one. Add 0.001 times the batch mean squared generated logit correction across coordinates, and clip gradient norm to 1. Use AdamW, weight decay 0.0001, lr 0.001, 20,000 steps per stage, β geometrically from 1 to 0.1 each stage. Include step zero and checkpoints every 1,000 steps. Choose by equal-family mean hard-mask validation nDCG@10 at 307. Freeze one recipe before final scoring. Three paired seeds 0,1,2. These are design defaults anchored to `src/hyperdime/training/retrieval_trainer.py`, RetrievalConfig, and `src/hyperdime/training/losses.py`, masked_retrieval_loss. The soft mask is a training surrogate only.
4. **Exact contract.** At evaluation use the hard boolean top-k in `src/hyperdime/selection/topk.py`, apply once, and exhaustively score the stored unchanged corpus with float64 accumulation and common deterministic tie order across arms (lower coordinate on selector ties, lower document row on score ties). Freeze the tie convention explicitly across backends. Verify exactly k ones, unchanged document checksum, equality with selected-coordinate summation, zero-correction mask equality and full-score equality at 1024. Hand fixture: q=(0.6,0.8), d=(0,1), selecting the second coordinate must score 0.8. Re-normalizing would score 1. No ANN result enters a scientific comparison (architecture invariants, ADR-0016 and ADR-0020).
5. **Cost.** Charge frozen document encoding separately as common setup. Time P0 descriptor construction including streamed covariance O(ND²) and its peak memory, P1 calibration encoding and exact O(|Q_calib|ND) scans, then O(Dh(r+1)) generation, serialization and cache loading. For each query measure encoding, base matvec, two factor matvecs (2Dr = 8,192 extra multiply-accumulates), prior, top-k, masking and exhaustive search separately. Theoretical selected-coordinate work Nk does not guarantee a wall-time saving on dense storage. Report hardware, batch size, cold/warm median and p95, resident bytes, and corpus size. Against full retrieval, break-even query count is the ceiling of incremental setup time divided by measured positive online saving; if saving ≤0, there is no break-even. Also report incremental time versus pooled S4, which uses the same k. Proposed G5: positive savings and break-even within a preregistered workload of 100,000 queries per corpus refresh. Sources: `docs/protocols.md`, Reporting, `docs/architecture.md`, G5, implementation plan E6.

## Evidence that would change the decision

**Controls.** Report full 1024 and MRL prefix-k in every retrieval table, variance-only, pooled L2S, pooled S4 and a matched global model. For the latter use the identical generator, but feed a fixed coordinate template from meta-train (the column-standardized M, frozen) at every episode and test; retain each target's true variance prior. From the identical pretrained base give this model the same second-stage optimization as the conditional model. Include a pooled S4 allowed to continue training its base for those updates. Freeze the strongest global comparator by validation before confirmation. Measure a foreign-descriptor control by substituting a fixed, hash-assigned meta-train donor's X only, leaving v_T untouched. Compare bias-only, a_k-only (pool X by column RMS, regress nine offsets to a and project nonnegative), and fixed-budget rank-four. Keep priors, steps, labels and evaluation queries paired. These controls distinguish added optimization, generic geometry, corpus identity and actual query-dependent correction (implementation plan risks R2/R6 and E3–E5, ADR-0013 sets 1–6).

**Primary estimand.** For each unseen parent family, average seed-paired query differences in exact nDCG@10 at 307 against the locked strongest global comparator, averaging environments equally within family. Report the equal-family mean and individual differences. The gain is also the absolute amount by which the gap to *any common reference* narrows. On development families only, report the fraction of a separately supervised in-domain gap recovered when the gap exceeds 0.01; otherwise report absolute differences. Training a specialist on labels of a meta-test corpus would breach the split, so no such denominator is required at confirmation. Secondary endpoints are the mean across the nine budgets, individual curves, linear-gain nDCG@10, MRR@10/100, Recall@100, mask overlap, frequency and importance entropy (protocols, Reporting; E1 payoff rows 1–3).

**Precision before unblinding.** The generalization unit is the independent parent family. Queries give paired precision *within* it; seeds and budgets are repeated measures. Freeze the family list, eligibility, negatives, splits, artifact hashes, arms and analysis before opening test judgments in a scoring-only process. Define a worthwhile mean gain of 0.005. For planning a superiority test versus zero, a hypothetical between-family SD of 0.010 implies about 32 independent families by the normal approximation ((1.96+0.84) × 0.010/0.005)² at two-sided 5% and 80% power. This SD is an assumption, not estimated from E1. Before locking sample size, simulate the actual decision rule with development-family heterogeneity, paired query residuals and seed averaging, including plausible larger variances and a true gain of 0.010. Target ≥80% probability of passing the *joint* gates at that alternative. If genuinely independent eligible collections are insufficient, report a pilot with intervals; extra shards cannot close that deficit (protocols, Environments and Significance; ADR-0008, Context).

**G4 and falsification.** Require a meta-validation mean gain ≥0.005 against the locked global model and positive correct-minus-donor mean before releasing the untouched meta-test once. For confirmation require mean gain ≥0.005, a positive lower 95% family-cluster bootstrap bound against global, and a positive lower bound against the donor. Resample whole families then paired queries within environments, carrying all arm/seed/budget observations together. Report paired query randomization within each corpus with Holm correction across the family of secondary contrasts. Treat both primary contrasts as necessary for the single success claim. Measure prior dominance, descriptor clipping and correction RMS for every family. If the upper 95% bound against global is below +0.005, retire this architecture for insufficient practical benefit. If swapping descriptors preserves gains within a preregistered equivalence band ±0.002 (paired 90% interval wholly inside), reject the claimed corpus-conditioning mechanism. If the bias-only or budget-only control is equivalent by that rule, retain the simpler selector. A wide interval crossing the thresholds leaves the mechanism unresolved. G5 failure separately ends the deployment claim. Record the decision even on failure and generate all reported tables from manifested result files (architecture G3–G5, protocols Reporting, ADR-0018 freeze procedure).
